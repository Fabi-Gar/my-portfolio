Our Story

Building a Better Future, One Donation at a Time

Founded in late 2022, Send A Miracle emerged from a shared passion for making a tangible difference in the lives of those in need. From humble beginnings, our organization has grown to become a beacon of hope for hundreds of families.

Through initiatives focused on providing essential resources like water filters, food, nutrient packets, and clothing, we've been able to touch the lives of many. As of now we have over 40 volunteers and we've helped 700+ people get access to basic needs.

Our First Project

The First Miracle

Send A Miracle organized a successful charity event in a Guatemalan village where we donated food, clothes, toys, and water filters to residents in need. The event helped provide relief to those facing hunger and poverty, while the water filters provide access to clean and safe drinking water.

Our Work

Miracles in Action

Five programs with one goal: erasing poverty, empowering lives.

Miracle Water

Access to clean water is essential for human health. Our water program provides impoverished communities with clean water, reducing waterborne diseases and improving overall health.

Miracle Meals

Providing regular access to healthy meals and preventing malnutrition, which can have severe health consequences, particularly for children in poverty-stricken communities.

Miracle Minds

Education is the most effective way to break the cycle of poverty. It equips individuals with knowledge and skills to improve their economic prospects and quality of life.

Miracle Medicare

Access to quality healthcare services that would otherwise be unavailable or unaffordable to individuals living in poverty. A basic human right for all.

Miracle Employment

Job training that breaks the cycle of poverty by providing new skills and knowledge to secure higher-paying jobs and increase income levels.
